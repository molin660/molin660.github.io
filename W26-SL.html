    Learning Goal 1: LITERACY – Technological Literacy

    Learn new technology and data governance tools!

    Action Plan 1

    The team uses data governance tools such as Informatica and Collibra which are new to me. Using software I previously have experience in such as Tableau, SQL, and Excel, I will be proactively finding opportunities to develop proficiency with these new tools as they are very applicable to the data governance domain.     Measure of Success 1

    I want to be able to say on my resume at the end of the term that I was able to be functionally proficient with these tools and understand the business value of why they are used in different ways.     After completing my work term, I can confidently say I gained a lot of experience with these tools. I also used Excel and SQL to manage and work with various data sources including ServiceNow, SharePoint, and Archer. These technologies were introduced to me in coursework, and I was able to expand on Continuous Improvement Initiatives in a real-world business setting.


    I also gained hands-on experience with the Data Governance platforms Collibra and Informatica, which taught me cloud-based data management systems and documentation skills. This exposure strengthened my technical literacy and helped me understand how these tools support enterprise data governance.
