From April to August, 2024, I had the honour of joining a one of a kind organization whose main business objective is to facilitate elections in Ontario which are, quite literally the backbone of our society.

My main reflections from this internship that I will bring into my future ambitions in interdisciplinary technical problem solving are:

3. Applying strategies for analyzing future risk preparedness.

The Office of the Chief Electoral Officer (Elections Ontario) About the employer

Elections Ontario (EO) is an independent, nonpartisan office of the Legislative Assembly of Ontario, responsible for administering provincial elections, by-elections, and referenda in Ontario. We are committed to making voting easy and accessible for all electors, while maintaining the integrity, security, and transparency of the electoral process.

My Team: The Provincial Registry of Electors My Role in the company (job description) The goals of the Register Division are to maintain the integrity and accuracy of Ontario’s voter registration lists. This is done through consolidating bulk data from external suppliers and internal sources, then standardizing and transforming databases via data curation techniques, to be updated into an enterprise management system.

The Register division handles provincial voter information and there were several large projects for the upcoming 2026 election to improve registration accuracy, verify data quality, maintain secure voter records and establish a more robust, transparent and accessible election experience for Ontarians.

My role involved automating data processing pipelines for voter registration records, coordinating with external data suppliers to ensure timely and accurate delivery of registration data, and creating documentation on best practices for maintaining data integrity.
